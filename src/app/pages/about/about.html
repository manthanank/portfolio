About Me

Software engineer passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences

Hello, I'm Manthan! 👋

Also known as manthanank, I'm a full-stack software engineer based in Karnataka, India, with 3.5+ years of experience building scalable web applications, microservices and AI-driven workflows.

I work mostly in Next.js, Angular, Node.js and Python on cloud-native infrastructure. My recent work covers integrations with dealer management systems, LLM vision pipelines and high-throughput SaaS dashboards. I hold a B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering from Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management.

Outside of work I write technical articles on Dev.to and maintain open learning guides in TheLearningHubOrg. I'm open to full-time roles and freelance projects, remote or on-site.
